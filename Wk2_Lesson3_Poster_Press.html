Leader’s Message

Write a three-sentence Mission Day message for the whole school. Use at least four focus words. Talk to us, say why, ask for action.

Sentence starters

• Talk to us: This Friday, our school … will …
• Say why: Every person has … , and …
• Ask for action: Please show your … by …
