My Letter

Write a letter to yourself in Year 7. What will be different? What are you looking forward to? What advice would you give? Aim for five focus words. When you’re done, press Proofread my letter and fix at least one slip.

Sentence starters

• Dear Year 7 me, right now I am in Year 6 and …
• By the time you read this, you will be at … school.
• I know you might feel … on the first day, but …
• Remember that your … is your responsibility, so …
• Every new club is an … — try …
• When things go wrong, use your … and …
• From, Year 6 me

Tap a word to drop it into your letter.

🔒 Write at least two sentences of your own first — then the worked example unlocks.
Worked example (one way to do it): Dear Year 7 me,
By now you’ve started secondary school. I bet you felt anxious on the first day — that’s normal! Keep your schedule in your pocket and pack your own bag, because being independent is your responsibility now. Say yes to every opportunity, even the scary ones. Your confidence will grow, and when things go wrong, use your resilience and try again.
