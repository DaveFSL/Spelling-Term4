04

Home Science

Think of changes you’ve seen at home. Explain one reversible and one irreversible change, using at least four focus words. What changed? Can it go back? Why?

Sentence starters

• At home, I saw … . This is a reversible change because …
• When … , it is an irreversible change, because a new substance …
• This is a physical / chemical change because …

Ideas: ice cubes in a drink · a steamy bathroom mirror · cooking pancakes · a rusty bike chain · sugar in a cup of tea

🔒 Write at least one sentence of your own first — then the worked example unlocks.
Worked example (one way to do it): After a hot shower, condensation forms on the cold bathroom mirror. This is a reversible, physical change because the droplets can turn back into water vapour by evaporation. When I cook pancakes, the batter goes through an irreversible chemical change — you can never turn a cooked pancake back into runny batter.
