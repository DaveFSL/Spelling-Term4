    03

    Honest Ad

    Invent a product (make up the brand name!) and write a short honest advertisement for it — facts only, no tricks. Use at least four focus words.

    Sentence starters

    • Introducing … — a … for every …
    • Each … comes with a … so you can …
    • We won’t tell you it’s a … — but it is …
    • Before you … , compare the price and keep your …

    Product ideas: a drink bottle · a school bag · a bike helmet · a pencil case · a torch

    🔒 Write at least one sentence of your own first — then the worked example unlocks.
    Worked example (one way to do it): Introducing the TrailTough drink bottle from Brightwater, a small Australian manufacturer. It holds 750 mL and keeps water cold for six hours. Every purchase comes with a two-year guarantee, so keep your receipt. We won’t pretend it’s a necessity — but if you already need a bottle, it’s a smart choice for any consumer. Facts you can check ✓ · No hurry, no “everyone has one” ✓ · Five focus words ✓

    Detective challenge (extension)

    Add a line about how your product is sustainable, or who the entrepreneur or manufacturer is behind it.
