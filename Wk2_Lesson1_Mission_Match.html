Case Notes

This week’s eight words come in four pairs. Each pair shows two sides of caring for others. Read each card, then come back whenever you need to.

1 · Compassion and charity

Compassion is feeling for someone who is hurting and wanting to help. Charity is giving to people in need — and an organisation that does this is also called a charity. In our faith, charity also means love (from the Latin caritas) — loving God and loving others.

Remember: Compassion is the feeling. Charity is the giving that follows.

2 · Generosity and volunteer

Generosity is being happy to give more than you have to. A volunteer is someone who chooses to help, without being paid.

Remember: Generosity is giving freely, whether things or time. A volunteer is a person who chooses to give their time.

3 · Justice and dignity

Justice is fairness — making sure every person gets what they rightly deserve. Dignity is the worth every person has because they are made in the image of God.

Remember: Justice is a fair go for all. Dignity is the worth every person has because they are made in the image of God.

4 · Community and solidarity

A community is a group of people who share a place and care for each other — like our school and parish. Solidarity is standing together with others, especially people who are doing it tough.

Remember: A community is the group. Solidarity is standing together.
