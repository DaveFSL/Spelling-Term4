Case Notes

Eight words for the move to Year 7, in three groups: what changes, how we manage it, and how it feels.

1 · What changes: secondary, schedule, independent

In Queensland, Year 7 is the start of secondary school (high school). You follow a schedule — a timetable of classes, rooms and times — and you become more independent: doing things for yourself without being reminded.

Remember: Secondary school = a new schedule and more independent learning.

2 · How we manage it: responsibility, opportunity, resilience

Your bag, planner and homework are your responsibility — your job to look after. New clubs and subjects are an opportunity — a chance to try something. Resilience is bouncing back when something goes wrong.

Remember: Take responsibility, grab each opportunity, and use resilience to bounce back.

3 · How it feels: anxious and confidence

Feeling anxious (worried or nervous) about a big change is normal. Naming the feeling helps. Confidence — believing you can do it — grows each time you have a go and it works out.

Remember: Anxious is a feeling, not a stop sign. Confidence grows with practice.
