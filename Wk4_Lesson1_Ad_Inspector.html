Case Notes

This week’s eight words come in four pairs. Every smart shopper knows them.

1 · Consumer and purchase

A consumer is a person who buys and uses goods and services — that’s you! To purchase something means to buy it. A purchase is also the thing you bought.

Remember: A consumer makes a purchase.

2 · Advertisement and persuasive

An advertisement (an ad) tries to sell you something. Ads use persuasive language — words chosen to convince you to think, feel or buy something.

Remember: An advertisement uses persuasive words to sell.

3 · Influence and necessity

A necessity is something you truly need, like food or shelter. A want is nice to have. Friends, ads and trends can influence — have an effect on — what we choose to buy.

Remember: A necessity is a need. Influence is what pushes our choices.

4 · Receipt and guarantee

A receipt is the slip that proves what you paid. A guarantee is a promise that a product will work — and that it will be fixed or replaced if it doesn’t.

Remember: Keep the receipt so you can use the guarantee.
